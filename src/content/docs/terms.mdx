## What Treasury Guild is

Treasury Guild is software that helps communities run Cardano multisig treasuries: workgroups and tasks, payouts and payments, call attendance, budgets, proposals and voting, minting, and related bookkeeping. It is not a bank, custodian, exchange, broker or investment advisor. It never holds your wallet keys or seed phrase. Communities decide their own membership, budgets and spending; Treasury Guild provides the tools.

## Your responsibilities

- Check every transaction before you sign it. Once it is on the Cardano blockchain it cannot be undone.
- Use the network that matches the community (mainnet or preprod). A community's network is fixed when it is created.
- You are responsible for the text you enter (proposal bodies, task titles, payment memos, call titles, and similar). Do not put private information in memos or other fields that may be published on-chain or shown to the community.

## Communities and admins

Admins and managers control membership, roles, budgets, treasuries, call platforms and announcements for their community. A community that captures Discord, Zoom or Meet attendance may record people who never signed into Treasury Guild, because that is how the community counts attendance (see the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy#what-treasury-guild-stores)). Spending decisions belong to the community and its signers, not to the operator of Treasury Guild.

## On-chain activity

Payouts, payments, mints and proposal anchors that go through Treasury Guild are Cardano transactions. Their outputs and metadata are public and permanent. Treasury Guild cannot reverse, edit or erase them. Workgroup names and any payment memo you enter can appear in that public record.

## Integrations

- **Discord.** When a community connects its server, the Treasury Guild bot may take short voice-channel attendance snapshots and post announcements the community configures. It does not join calls to listen, and it does not read Discord messages. Personal Discord linking uses the `identify` scope only.
- **Zoom and Google Meet.** A community may connect a host account so attendance of its listed or linked meetings can be recorded. Scope and retention match the [privacy policy](/docs/privacy).
- **GitHub.** A community may install the GitHub App to turn issues into tasks and write back payment status. Linking your own GitHub account is optional.
- **AI assistants.** An assistant you approve can read community data you can see as a member, and (if you grant write permissions) can make changes only after a preview you confirm through that assistant. Assistants never sign, submit or vote on transactions for you. What the assistant does with data it receives is governed by that assistant's own terms and privacy policy.

Third-party services (Discord, GitHub, Zoom, Google, Koios, Pinata, CoinGecko, hosting providers and others named in the privacy policy) have their own terms. Treasury Guild is not responsible for those services' availability or decisions.
