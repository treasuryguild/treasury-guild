A **community** is the top-level organisation in Treasury Guild: a DAO, guild, working group or open-source collective. It owns one or more [treasuries](/docs/treasury), has [members with roles](/docs/members-roles), and is divided into [workgroups](/docs/workgroups-boards), each with its own board and optional budget.

## Discover

**Discover** lists every **public** community plus any private community you already belong to. Communities on your wallet's network are listed first; the others are dimmed with a note ("Runs on preprod; switch your wallet to a preprod account to take part"). Each card shows the name, description, a **Preprod** badge for test communities and a **private** badge for private communities you belong to.

- **Join** — appears on public communities you are not yet a member of. One click makes you a member.
- **Open** — takes you to a community you already belong to.

You can browse Discover without signing in, but joining requires a signed-in wallet.

## Joining a community

You can join through the directory, an invitation or work you take part in:

1. **Public communities:** click **Join** on Discover or on the Welcome page, or **Join community** on the community's own page ("You are not a member of this community yet.").
2. **Private communities, invited:** an admin adds your wallet address on the Members page. Ask them for the community link.
3. **Private communities, via GitHub:** [link your GitHub account](/docs/profile#linking-a-github-account). You automatically join any community whose boards have tasks assigned to your GitHub username, now or later.
4. **Call check-in:** a manager's [check-in link or code](/docs/calls#checking-in) also invites your signed-in wallet to the community, including a private one.
5. **Discord capture:** if you linked your Discord account, being captured in a community's rewarded voice call adds your wallet as a member.
6. **Treasury base signer or workgroup lead:** an admin listing your wallet as a base signer of the community's **first (primary)** treasury adds you as an admin; listing you on a later treasury adds you as a member with spend rights for that wallet only. Making you a workgroup lead adds you as a member. Recovery signers and a co-signer must already be members and are not made admins.

New members get the **member** role. See [Members & roles](/docs/members-roles) for what that allows.

## Creating a community

Open **New community** from the sidebar. You need to be signed in.

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Shown everywhere. |
| **Slug** | The short name in the address (`/c/your-slug`), also written into every on-chain payout record. Filled in automatically from the name (changing the name again resets it, so edit the slug last). 2–63 lowercase letters, digits and hyphens, not starting with a hyphen; must be unique ("Slug already taken"). |
| **Description** | Optional, shown on Discover and the overview. |
| **Cardano network** | **Mainnet** or **Preprod testnet**. Preselected to match your connected wallet's network; if you pick the other one, a note reminds you that you will need an account on that network to run its treasury. |
| **Visibility** | **Private** (the default) or **Public**. Private is invite-only; public communities appear on Discover and anyone signed in can join. Changeable later on the Members page. |

<Callout tone="warning" title="The network cannot be changed later">
A community's network is fixed at creation. Every treasury, payout and mint in it lives on that network. Create a preprod community to experiment, and a separate mainnet community for real funds.
</Callout>

The creator becomes the community's **owner**.

## Community overview

The overview (`/c/your-slug`) is the community's home. The title shows the community name, a **Preprod** badge if applicable, and your role. Admins also see a short **Setup** list of unfinished [Integrations](/docs/integrations) steps (Discord, payout Discord, GitHub, call platforms); dismissing an item hides it on Overview only.

**Cards** link to each section:

| Card | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Budgets | Plan workgroup and treasury budgets and see spending. See [Budgets](/docs/budgets). |
| Proposals | Vote on budgets and community decisions. See [Proposals & voting](/docs/governance). |
| Treasury | Multisig wallets, balances and recent transactions. See [Treasury](/docs/treasury). |
| Payday | Batch-pay audited tasks. See [Payday](/docs/payday). |
| Payments | Pay addresses, members or another treasury. See [Payments](/docs/payments). |
| Calls | Collect attendance and create reward tasks. See [Call attendance](/docs/calls). |
| Mint | Create community tokens and NFTs. See [Minting tokens](/docs/mint). |
| Reconcile | Match refunds sent back to the treasury. See [Incoming & reconciliation](/docs/reconciliation). |
| Funding sources | Committed funding against what arrived. See [Funding sources](/docs/funding). |
| Integrations | Discord, Zoom, Meet, payout Discord destinations, and status of other connections. See [Integrations](/docs/integrations). |
| GitHub | Connect repos and project boards. See [GitHub integration](/docs/github). |
| Members | Roles, visibility and workgroup leads. See [Members & roles](/docs/members-roles). |

**Workgroups & projects** lists every workgroup as a card. Click a card to open its board. Cards show whether the workgroup is an app board or a GitHub project, its description, and **Paid from** when payouts are restricted to one treasury. Tick **Show archived** to include archived workgroups.

Admins also see:

- an **Edit details** button on each workgroup (name, description, payment wallet, archive or delete), and
- unless the community decides new workgroups by vote, a card to add a new workgroup: type a name, choose **App board** or **GitHub project**, and click **Add**. When it does, the card reads "New workgroups are created by a community vote." for everyone and offers **Propose a workgroup** to those allowed to propose (see [Proposal types](/docs/governance#proposal-types)).

How to run workgroups and boards is covered in [Workgroups & boards](/docs/workgroups-boards).
