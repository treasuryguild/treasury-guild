Recognition is an **optional** module for celebrating higher-quality work. It is off until an admin turns it on under **Recognition** in the community settings. It never changes payouts, budgets, who may sign a treasury transaction, or membership roles.

## Turning it on

Admins open **Recognition** in the sidebar (settings). Enable the module, pick a season cadence (**quarter** or **month**), and set how many endorsements each lead or admin may grant per season. Presets (**Quiet**, **Standard**, **Loud**) fill sensible knobs; you can still edit them afterward.

Turning recognition **off** hides the season board and refuses new endorsements. Past events stay in the community's history.

## Endorsing paid work

On a **paid** or **reconciled** task, admins and leads of that workgroup can **Endorse** each assignee's wallet from the task dialog. Call attendance tasks cannot be endorsed. You cannot endorse yourself. Each assignee may have one active endorsement per task; a mistaken endorsement can be **Revoked** (by the grantor or an admin).

A short optional note explains why the work stood out. Each actor has a season budget of endorsements — remaining count is shown in the dialog.

## Peer kudos

When **Allow peer kudos** is on, any member who meets the membership-age rule may give **Kudos** on a paid task assignee (separate from lead endorsements, with a lower score weight). Reciprocal kudos in a short window do not count toward the season score.

## Season board and score

The season board (linked from Recognition settings when the module is on) lists wallets and their season score. Score is built only from recognition events: **3** points per active endorsement, **0.25** per counting peer kudos. ADA paid never enters the formula.

Tiers: **Noted** (1–2), **Craft** (3–5), **Exemplar** (6+). The leaderboard ranking is optional (`Show season leaderboard`); when off, the page still lists people with scores without numbered places.

## Recognized contributors as voters

With **Offer “Recognized contributors” as a proposal electorate** on, admins may choose that electorate under [Governance settings](/docs/governance#governance-settings) for a proposal kind. At voting open, members who meet the minimum recognition score in the look-back window (and the usual membership-age rule) are snapshotted — still one member, one vote. If recognition is off or that option is unchecked, the electorate is empty, so only turn it on when the module is in use.

## Season badge drafts

Admins on the season board can **Download badge drafts** for Exemplar wallets: JSON metadata suggestions for NFTs. Mint them under the treasury policy on [Mint](/docs/mint) like any other NFT (several per transaction). Recognition never auto-mints or spends treasury funds for badges.

## What recognition is not

- Not a substitute for marking a task payable or paying it
- Not token-gated membership or admin rights
- Not a claim on the treasury
